# Product

## One line

Search a candidate and see what they have actually said, word for word, about specific climate issues, starting with data centers. Each quote comes with a clip that starts at the moment they said it. No score, no rating, no ranking. You get the evidence, or "no statement found."

## Who it's for

**A voter who doubts candidates' climate claims.** This voter has heard "I support clean energy" from everyone and wants to know what a candidate has actually committed to on a concrete issue, such as whether new data centers must bring their own clean power or who pays for the grid upgrades.

## What they do today (the before)

1. Open each candidate's campaign site. They find slogans and issue pages that the campaign wrote and edits, with no dates and no exact wording.
2. Search news coverage. They find reporters' paraphrases, often of a single line, without the conditions the candidate attached.
3. Find debate, forum, and town-hall videos on YouTube, C-SPAN, local access TV, or newspaper sites. These videos often run 60 to 120 minutes. They scrub through them looking for the topic.
4. Repeat for every candidate on the ballot.
5. Give up, or decide from a headline or an ad.

The result: hours of work per candidate, and the voter still can't be sure a quote isn't edited or taken out of context.

## What Firsthand does (the after)

1. Pick a candidate and a topic from a fixed checklist.
2. See **every** matching moment, oldest first. Each one shows the exact words, the transcript lines just before and after, the date, the venue, the publisher, and a link that opens the official video at that second.
3. See how much we searched: "8 videos, 11.5 hours."
4. If nothing matches, see "No statement found in the 8 videos we searched" and a neutral question to ask the campaign office.
5. Switch to another candidate. The page format is identical.

## What it deliberately does not do

- Score, grade, rank, or color-code candidates.
- Summarize or paraphrase positions.
- Infer a position from silence.
- Host or edit video.
- Track users or store searches.

## Topics (v0 checklist)

These are defined in `config/topics.json`:

- Requires new clean power for data centers
- Protects ratepayers from data center costs
- Water and energy use disclosure
- Moratorium (for or against)
- Local approval or community input

## Hackathon context

The MTC hackathon, Climate Good track, sponsored by the Rutgers Office of Climate Action. We have 3 people and less than 8 hours. Success means a working demo with 3 to 5 real candidates across parties and 15 to 30 hand-verified videos.
